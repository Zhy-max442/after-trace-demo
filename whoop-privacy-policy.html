Privacy Policy - Muse Personal

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This application is for personal use only by its owner.

It accesses WHOOP data (profile, recovery, sleep, workouts, cycles, and body measurements) on a read-only basis, with the owner's consent via OAuth.

Data is used solely to provide personal insights to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The owner can revoke access at any time in their WHOOP account settings under Connected Apps.

Contact: the application owner, via the contact email listed in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard for this application.
